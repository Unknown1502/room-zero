# Differentiators

This document separates what is distinctive about ROOM ZERO from what is shared with other games. It makes no claim that any mechanic is unprecedented. The research behind it is in `competitive-research.md`, which also states how limited that research was.

## What is distinctive in combination

1. **One graph serves player, puzzle and enemy.** Rearranging rooms is not only how the player gets somewhere. It is the only way to influence the enemy, because the enemy has no navigation other than the doorway connections the player has just made or broken. We found no VR title that advertises this specific coupling, and we did not search exhaustively.
2. **Architecture moved by hand at courier scale.** The player does not push a button that plays a room animation. They hold a brass grip on a rail and drag it, and a room many times their size follows with weight. Two-handed seams require bracing against the house with one hand while pulling with the other.
3. **An enemy with a compulsion instead of senses.** The Surveyor does not see or hear the player in the usual stealth-game way. It is drawn to the newest joint in the house, then to the letter. The player manipulates it by building, which is the same verb used for everything else.
4. **The count.** When the Surveyor plans a route it knocks once per doorway it must pass. The threat level is a number the player can hear and, through a visual twin, see.
5. **A diegetic object that is the whole interface.** The envelope is objective, hint system, checkpoint record and settings menu.
6. **Scale inversion as the reveal.** The whole chapter is oversized. The destination is the only room built at the player's size.

## What is not new

| Element | Prior art we are aware of |
|---|---|
| Impossible, non-Euclidean interiors in VR | Hotel Infinity, Sommad: Impossible Spaces, Tea For God |
| Being small in a threatening oversized world | The Little Nightmares series, including its 2026 VR entry |
| Rearranging rooms or tiles to make a path | ROOMS: The Unsolvable Puzzle, Blue Prince, Carto, Gorogoa (from general knowledge, not verified in this research pass) |
| Recursive or scale-based spatial puzzles in VR | A Fisherman's Tale |
| Hand-manipulated puzzle mechanisms | The Room VR and many others |
| A pursuer the player must outwit rather than fight | Common across horror games |

## Differentiation from the tonal reference

Little Nightmares is a tonal reference for scale contrast, silhouette and sound-led tension. The following are deliberately different.

| Aspect | Little Nightmares | ROOM ZERO |
|---|---|---|
| Perspective | Third-person diorama; first-person in the VR entry | First person, seated |
| Core verb | Platforming, hiding, grabbing objects | Moving sections of the building |
| Protagonist | A named child in a signature raincoat | An unnamed adult courier seen only as two gloved hands and a sleeve cuff |
| Setting | A vessel, a city, a resort | A boarding house of separable rooms hung in a void |
| Enemies | Grotesque, hungry human caricatures | One non-human caretaker of plaster and brass whose head is an open ring; it measures and does not eat |
| Themes | Hunger, consumption, childhood fear | Return, address, belonging, the labour of putting a place back together |
| Failure | Capture and death | Being measured and carried back to the last postmark |
| Enemy model | Authored patrols and chases | A state machine on a graph the player edits |

Hard rules for production are in the originality checklist in `03-art-and-audio/art-direction-bible.md`. No yellow raincoat, no child protagonist, no paper-bag or sack masks, no chefs, teachers, doctors or other caricatured adults, no ship or ocean framing, no hunger motif, and no music-box lullaby.

## Honest limits

- A five to seven minute chapter cannot show how far the mechanic scales. Six seams is a proof, not a campaign.
- Authored vantage points trade freedom of movement for comfort and for a clean graph. Some players will want to walk.
- A WebXR build on a mobile headset cannot match the visual density of the native games listed above. The art direction is chosen to look deliberate within that budget.
