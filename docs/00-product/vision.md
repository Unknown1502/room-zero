# Vision

## One line

A tiny night courier rearranges an impossible boarding house, one seam at a time, to deliver a letter to a room that no longer exists, while the house's caretaker walks the same doorways the courier opens.

## The promise

In five to seven minutes, with nothing but your hands, you will move a building, be hunted by something that obeys rules you can learn, and arrive somewhere that changes what you think the hunt was.

## What the player should feel, and what produces it

| Feeling | Produced by |
|---|---|
| Small | The house is built at about three times human scale. Only the brass fittings, the letter and one room are your size |
| Curious | Rooms are visible across the void before they are reachable. The destination is in view, as a far-off lit lantern, from the second minute |
| Clever | Each puzzle has one idea that is seen rather than guessed: the door that works is the wrong door; to dock a room you must first make room for it |
| Vulnerable | The Surveyor is slow, enormous and never stops. It tells you how many doorways away it is |
| Relieved | A seam closes with a deep thud and the count outside goes quiet |
| Invested | The letter is in your hand from the first second, is your save file, your hint system and your menu, and is in your own handwriting |

## Design pillars

1. **One mechanic, fully owned.** Seam manipulation carries traversal, puzzles and the enemy. Nothing else is added until that is excellent.
2. **The graph is the game.** One deterministic graph of rooms and doorways is the truth for the player's route, the puzzles' goals and the enemy's path. If a doorway is open for you, it is open for it.
3. **Fair dread.** The Surveyor never teleports, never spawns behind you and never cheats. Its two rules fit in one sentence each. Fear comes from knowing where it is.
4. **Hands, seated, no nausea.** Everything is done with tracked hands from a chair. You never glide, the room you are in never moves, and the camera is never taken from you.
5. **Scale is the art direction.** A believable oversized world in aged wood, plaster and brass, lit by few warm lamps and cold moonlight. No neon, no floating UI.

## Anti-goals

These would make the project a failure even if it shipped.

- A Little Nightmares imitation. That series is a tonal reference only. See `differentiators.md` and the originality checklist in `03-art-and-audio/art-direction-bible.md`.
- A physics sandbox where rooms can be thrown anywhere. The house is authored and constrained.
- An enemy that feels random or scripted to appear behind the player.
- A large game. One complete chapter is the product.
- A tech demo with programmer art presented as finished.
- Any claim of headset performance that was only measured in the emulator.

## Scope of this product

One chapter, *The Unreturned Letter*: eleven room modules, six seams, one enemy, one ending, restartable. A second chapter is not planned in this package and nothing here depends on one.
