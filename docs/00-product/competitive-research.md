# Competitive research

## Method and its limits

This is a short desk review done on 2026-11-16, not a market study. Each entry is marked by how it was established.

- **Searched**: confirmed by a web search during this pass. Sources are listed at the end.
- **Recalled**: from general knowledge. Not re-verified here. Check before using in any public claim.

Nobody on this project has played these titles for the purpose of this review. Before any marketing statement about uniqueness, a fuller search of the Meta Horizon Store, Steam, SideQuest and itch.io is required (Q-21 in `07-delivery/unresolved-questions.md`).

## Findings

1. Impossible interiors and oversized-world dread are both proven on Quest. Neither is a differentiator alone.
2. Rearranging rooms to make a path is an established puzzle form on flat screens and exists in at least one VR title by recollection. We must not call it new.
3. We did not find a VR game in which the player's rearrangement of rooms is also the enemy's only navigation data. That is our clearest claim, and it is a claim about what we found, not about what exists.
4. The closest tonal neighbour shipped on Quest this year. Visual and narrative distance from it is a product requirement (REQ-ART-002), not a preference.
5. Short, complete, authored experiences are well received when the one idea is strong. That supports the scope.
